# Privacy

Omebly Downloader keeps everything on your computer.

- **Your downloads, history and settings** are saved only in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Omebly Downloader` on your PC.
- **Sign-ins:** the app never asks for a password. When you download from a site you are signed in to, the
  extension passes that site's cookies to the app, which sends them only to that same site while the download
  runs and deletes its copy when the download ends.
- **The only request to Omebly** is the update check (`https://api.omebly.com/downloads/downloader/latest.json`),
  made when the app opens or when you click "Check now". It carries nothing about you or what you download.
  Like any web request, the server sees your IP address; its logs are not used to identify anyone.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics.** Nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere.
- **Reviews:** after a few downloads the app asks once whether you would like to leave a review. It is never
  required; "Not now" or "Write a review" ends the question for good, and only "Write a review" opens a web page.
