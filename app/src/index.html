Add Omebly Downloader to your browser

One time only, about a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. After this, updates come by themselves.

  1. Open the extensions page. Click Copy, then paste it into your browser's address bar and press Enter.
    chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode. The switch is at the top right (in Edge, at the bottom left).
  3. Click Load unpacked. Click Copy below, paste it into the Folder box of the window that opens, then click Select Folder.
  4. Pin it. Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin the green Omebly button.
